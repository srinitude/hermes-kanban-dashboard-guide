Start with the diagnostic, then act

  1. The attention strip appears only while a card has an active diagnostic. Its color comes from the most severe finding.
  2. Click Show to sort the affected cards by severity, then open the one you want. Dismiss removes the strip until the next page load.
  3. The drawer gives you the diagnostic title, detail, data, and suggested action. Read all four before changing the task.
  4. Use the action that fits the finding. Hermes may offer to reclaim, reassign, unblock, focus the comment box, copy a CLI command, or open the relevant docs. If a future release adds an unknown action, the drawer disables it instead of failing.
Do not reclaim by habit. Check Worker log and Run history first. Reclaim only when the worker is dead, stuck, or no longer owns work you want to preserve.
