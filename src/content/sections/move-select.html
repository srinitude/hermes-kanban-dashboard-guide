Move one card or change many at once

One card

  • Drag the card to another column. On touch, press and drag with one finger.
  • Click the card, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to open the drawer.
  • Drag it to the trash zone for permanent deletion. Hermes asks first.

Select cards

  • Use a card checkbox.
  • Use Cmd or Ctrl plus click to add or remove one.
  • Use Shift plus click to select a visible range.
  • Use a column header checkbox for that column.
  • Press Esc on a focused card to clear the current selection and select that card.

The bulk bar

After you select at least one card, the bar can move them to Todo or Ready, block, unblock, complete, archive, delete, set priority, reassign or unassign, select every visible card, or clear the selection. Drag a selected card to move the whole selected group.

Reassigning active work: turn on Reclaim first only when you intend to stop current claims before changing the profile. Bulk Complete asks for one required completion summary. Bulk Delete is permanent.
